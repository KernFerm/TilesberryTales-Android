Privacy Policy

Tileberry Tales: Mahjong Adventure

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Overview

Tileberry Tales: Mahjong Adventure is designed to work entirely offline. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, purchases, or telemetry, and it does not request runtime internet access.

Information the app stores

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Optional profile nicknames and avatars
  • Sensory and accessibility preferences
  • Game statistics and achievements
  • Parental settings
  • The active game board and restoration data

This information is stored in Android DataStore or app-private saved state. It is not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

Parental PIN

If a parental PIN is created, the app stores only a salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 verifier. The original PIN is not stored.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not request or collect a user's age, date of birth, email address, telephone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, camera or microphone data, advertising identifiers, or other personal information.

Sharing and third-party services

The app does not sell, share, or transmit personal information. It does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud-account services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. All profiles, preferences, and game progress remain locally on the device.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until it is removed through the app, Android app-data controls, or by uninstalling the app. Guest statistics are not persisted. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app.

Security

Stored information is kept within Android's app-private storage. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, but the app minimizes risk by keeping its information on the device and not transmitting it.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Tileberry Tales GitHub issues page.
